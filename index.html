How to change your nickname

  1. 01
    Prepare: Copy your token from the browser game network request, paste it here and type your desired name.
  2. 02
    Refresh: Refresh the Fortzone game tab.
  3. 03
    Execute: Click EXECUTE INJECTION in this tool.
  4. 04
    Open game: Switch back to the game tab and let it load — your injected name will appear.
  5. 05
    Stop: Return here and click ABORT once the name has locked in.
💡 Reset original name: Refresh the game page WITHOUT pressing EXECUTE.
